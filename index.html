Spending on the spot

Typical resort prices per person in euros, winter 2026/27 estimates. Rentals are counted for each ski day, at standard adult level. Beginner classes are group lessons of about 2–3 hours a day; their cost is shared out over the whole group in the per-person total. Groceries are for a self-catering day (breakfast, snacks and one home-cooked meal). Set your plan and the totals update, including the ranking.

Green marks the cheapest destination in each column. Prices come from national cost-of-living averages (Numbeo, 2026) with a resort mark-up, and from published 2025/26 day-pass prices. Rental prices are walk-in rates for standard equipment; booking online with Skiset, Intersport or Hervis usually saves 20–40%, and a week often costs 6 days. Group classes run in local languages plus English or German; over New Year they fill up, so book with the ski school early (ESF in France, Skischule in Germany and Austria, szkoła narciarska in Poland, lyžařská škola in Czechia). Ski clothing is rented mostly in the larger shops, so reserve it ahead. Poland, Czechia and Romania use their own currencies, and cards are accepted almost everywhere; carry some cash for mountain huts.

Making sure there are no sofa beds

  • On Airbnb, open “Where you'll sleep”: every bedroom should show a double, queen or single beds. Skip listings that count a “sofa bed” or “floor mattress” as a sleeping place.
  • On Booking.com, look at the per-room bed list. “Sofa bed” in any bedroom is a no.
  • Local words for sofa bed: PL rozkładana sofa / kanapa, SK rozkladací gauč, RO canapea extensibilă, DE/IT Schlafcouch / divano letto.
  • For New Year, hosts often require 5–7 nights. Ask before you give up on a listing that shows no availability.

Message to send hosts

Why these destinations

  • Zakopane (PL): the classic Tatra New Year, with the biggest party and the biggest crowds. Easiest from Paris and Berlin via Kraków.
  • High Tatras (SK): the same mountains on the quieter Slovak side. The TEŽ electric railway links Poprad, Starý Smokovec, Tatranská Lomnica and Štrbské Pleso, so a car is never needed.
  • Sinaia / Bușteni (RO) (archived): Bucegi mountains on the Bucharest–Brașov main line. By far the easiest and cheapest for Chișinău, with a sleeper train straight to Bucharest.
  • San Candido / Innichen (IT) (archived), Alta Pusteria, Dolomites: a town with its own railway station, a ski slope at the edge of town, a gondola with its own train stop at Versciaco, and the Tre Cime area next door. The Dolomites benchmark, and the most expensive option.
  • Oberstdorf (DE): shortest average journey. Train all the way from Paris, Strasbourg and Berlin, and Wizz Air flies Chișinău → Memmingen, 1h20 away by train.
  • Krkonoše (CZ): Prague is reachable directly from every city (Berlin by train in 4h30), and cottage prices are close to Slovak levels. The mountains are lower, so snow is less certain.
  • Bad Gastein (AT): one direct train from Munich for Paris, Strasbourg and Berlin; Chișinău flies to Vienna. Big ski area and thermal spas, at Alpine prices.
  • Auron / Isola 2000 (FR): all four cities fly direct to Nice, and a €1.50 bus reaches the slopes in about 2h. Shortest average journey of all. Southern snow can vary.
  • Chamonix (FR) (archived): Geneva is a train from Strasbourg and a direct flight from Paris and Berlin. The most famous mountain, and the priciest housing.
  • Les Arcs (FR) (archived): best for Paris (direct TGV and a funicular to the slopes), but long for Berlin and Chișinău.
  • Piešťany was suggested, but it is a spa town in the lowlands of western Slovakia at about 160 m, without real mountains. The High Tatras are the Slovak mountain choice.

Calendar notes

  • 30 Dec 2026 is a Wednesday, 1 Jan 2027 a Friday, 2 Jan a Saturday, 6 Jan a Wednesday.
  • For 1–6 Jan: Austrian and some German hosts rent Saturday to Saturday, so a Friday arrival may need a request. In Romania, New Year packages often run to 2 Jan, so a 1 Jan arrival can overlap them.
  • French school holidays run 19 Dec 2026 – 4 Jan 2027. French resorts rent Saturday to Saturday: 26 Dec–2 Jan is peak, 2–9 Jan is one of the cheapest weeks of the winter.
  • 6 January (Epiphany) is a public holiday in Poland, Slovakia and Italy: slopes and trains are busy until the evening of the 6th.
  • 1–2 January are public holidays in Romania; Chișinău travellers celebrating Orthodox Christmas on 7 January may prefer the later dates.
  • Both date options share 2–5 January. If the group splits, a place booked 30 Dec–6 Jan (7 nights) covers everyone and meets most minimum stays.
  • Book trains as soon as sales open: DB/ÖBB sell about 6 months ahead, PKP Intercity about 30 days, CFR about 30–60 days.
