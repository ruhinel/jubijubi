# Winter Summit Planner

A single-page tool comparing car-free New Year mountain trips (30 Dec 2026 – 5 Jan 2027, 1 – 6 Jan 2027 or 2 – 6 Jan 2027) for a group leaving from Paris, Strasbourg, Berlin and Chișinău.

Destinations: Zakopane (PL), High Tatras – Starý Smokovec / Tatranská Lomnica (SK), Sinaia / Bușteni (RO), San Candido / Innichen (Dolomites, IT), plus three shorter-travel options: Oberstdorf (DE), Krkonoše – Špindlerův Mlýn (CZ) and Bad Gastein (AT).

Open `index.html` in a browser. Set travellers per city, pick the dates, weight your priorities, and type real housing quotes to replace the estimates. All prices are estimates compiled in September 2026.
